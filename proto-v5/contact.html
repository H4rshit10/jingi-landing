03 Before you write

Questions we are usually asked first.

  • 01How long does incorporation take?
    It depends on the structure and the approvals your business needs. A private limited company is usually registered within a few weeks; the licences that follow take longer. You get a dated plan for your own case at the first meeting, not a generic estimate.
  • 02Can we talk before we have decided anything?
    Yes, and earlier is better. The structure you choose decides most of what follows, and it is easier to choose well before a timetable exists.
  • 03Do you take one-off work?
    Yes. A single contract review or one compliance question is a normal request, alongside the continuing engagements.
  • 04Who will we actually deal with?
    One bilingual point of contact for the whole engagement, backed by the named specialist for each function. Their names are on the About page.
