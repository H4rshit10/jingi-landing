About JINGI

Who answers when your head office calls.

JINGI Corporate Solutions was set up in Gurugram to be the one number a Japanese company dials about India: a Japanese representative director, and Indian specialists in law, people and accounts who work as one office.

日本人代表と、法務・人事・会計のインド人専門家が、ひとつのチームとしてお応えします。
Tokyo · where the question starts Gurugram · where it is answered

01 The name

Two readings, one standard.

JINGI is written two ways in Japanese, and the company is named for both.

仁義
Jin-gi · sincerity, integrity

The duty you owe to people who have placed their trust in you. It is the standard we hold ourselves to with clients, and with the officials, landlords and candidates we deal with on your behalf.

神器
Jingi · the instruments of the craft

The tools that make a thing possible. Read this way, the name is a promise of competence: expertise applied properly, so that what you asked for actually works on the ground.

02 From the director

Closing the distance with people, not advice.

When a Japanese company looks at India, the opportunity is not the difficult part. The distance is: in language, in custom, in what a Tuesday afternoon actually looks like at the tax office.

We founded JINGI to close that distance with people rather than advice. Our promise is plain. We speak your language, we do the work ourselves in India, and we are still here in the years after the company is formed.

Hisako Sugawara Representative Director, JINGI Corporate Solutions 菅原 久子
