Service 01

India business entry

インド進出支援

Choosing the entity, forming it, and securing the licences and approvals that let it operate.

Most delays in an India entry are not legal; they are sequencing. A bank account needs a registration that needs a director that needs a document from Japan. We run that sequence for you, in order, and tell you in advance what will be asked of head office and when.

What we do

  • Entity selection: subsidiary, liaison office or joint venture
  • Company incorporation and statutory registrations
  • Land acquisition and site selection
  • Factory licensing and approvals
  • Incentive applications
  • Government and regulatory liaison
  • Bank account, first hires and go-live

What your head office receives

  • 01A structure recommendation with the reasoning, in Japanese, before anything is filed.
  • 02A registered company with PAN, GST and every statutory registration in place.
  • 03Licences, approvals and a bank account, ready for the first invoice.
  • 04A dated go-live checklist, with who owes what to whom.

Case · Manufacturing

Situation

A Japanese manufacturer needed an Indian entity and a factory, with no local base to start from.

What JINGI did

Incorporation, land and licensing, and the government liaison, end to end.

Result

Entity registered and factory operating on the agreed schedule.

Service 02

Legal & compliance

法務・コンプライアンス

Agreements drafted to your standards, obligations mapped, and filing dates watched so nothing lapses.

Indian labour and company law changes often and is enforced locally. We keep a single compliance picture for your entity, so the question "are we compliant?" has a current answer rather than a nervous one.

What we do

  • Contract drafting and review, in both languages
  • Labour-law compliance
  • Compliance mapping and advisory
  • Legal consulting on day-to-day questions
  • Risk prevention and dispute mitigation

What your head office receives

  • 01A compliance map: every law and filing that applies to your entity, and its date.
  • 02Bilingual contracts and policies, reviewed against head-office standards.
  • 03A calendar of obligations that we monitor, not you.
  • 04Early warning, in Japanese, when a regulation that affects you changes.

Case · Legal support

Situation

Complex contracts and changing labour regulation left a subsidiary carrying legal risk it could not see.

What JINGI did

Contract drafting and review, compliance advisory and a plan to reduce exposure.

Result

Exposure reduced and the operation kept compliant through the changes.

A subsidiary is only as reliable as the people in it. We hire for the role and for the relationship with head office, then run payroll, appraisals and policy so the team you built is still there next year.

What we do

  • Workforce planning and compensation bands
  • Sourcing and screening, including Japanese-speaking talent
  • Employment contracts, onboarding and payroll setup
  • Labour and HR management
  • HR system and policy development

What your head office receives

  • 03Payroll and statutory benefits set up correctly from the first month.
  • 04HR rules in both languages that your team will actually follow.
